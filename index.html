HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP
Move: WASD · Jump: Space · Sprint: Shift
Look: Click world · Release: Esc
Attack/Break: Left Click · Place/Use: Right Click
Hotbar: 1-9 or Mouse Wheel · Chat: T · Players: Tab
Fly (BuildHub): F

MOBILE
Joystick (bottom-left) to move
Drag right side to look
Tap screen to place a block
Hold screen for ~0.25s to break blocks
JUMP button, RUN toggle, CHAT button

TNT RUN
Blocks crumble when stepped on. Fall in the void and you're out.

SKYWARS
Chests on your island, middle loot, void death. Map blocks cannot be broken.

CREATIVE BUILD
Flat plot. Press F to fly. You can place and break blocks while flying.

BUILD BATTLE (2-5 players)
Everyone gets a plot and the same random theme. Build for 5 minutes, then vote for the best build. Most votes wins!
